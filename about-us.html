Corporate Heritage

The Flag Carrier & Pioneer of Asian Aviation

Philippine Airlines (PAL) is the flag carrier of the Philippines and Asia's first commercial airline. Flying nonstop from key hubs in Manila, Cebu, Clark, and Davao to over 32 domestic and 40 international destinations across the globe, PAL carries the warmth, hospitality, and resilience of the Filipino spirit.

From its pioneering beginnings in 1941 to supporting wartime missions, PAL played an instrumental role in rebuilding the nation. In the post-war era, the airline forged historic transpacific air links connecting Manila to San Francisco, Honolulu, and European capitals like London.

Today, PAL operates a modern long-range fleet of Airbus A350s, Boeing 777s, and fuel-efficient A321neos, continually innovating digital services, cabin comfort, and heartfelt customer care.
